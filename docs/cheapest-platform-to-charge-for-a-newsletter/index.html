Cheapest platform to charge for a newsletter

Short answer: for a small paid list, Substack is cheapest until about $90 a month in revenue, Buttondown ($9/mo at 1,000 subscribers) is the cheapest flat-fee plan, with MailerLite Comfort ($19/mo) next. These figures exclude card processing, which every platform passes on.

    FAQ

    What is the cheapest platform to charge for a newsletter?

    Substack has no monthly fee but keeps 10% of subscription revenue. Among flat-price platforms, Buttondown's Basic plan ($9/mo at 1,000 subscribers) takes 0% and includes paid subscriptions, followed by MailerLite Comfort ($19/mo). Card processing applies everywhere.

    When does Substack's 10% cost more than a flat-fee platform?

    When your monthly subscription revenue passes the plan price divided by 10%. For Buttondown at $9 that is about $90/mo; for beehiiv Lite at $59 it is about $590/mo.
