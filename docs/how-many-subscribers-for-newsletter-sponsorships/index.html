How many subscribers do you need for newsletter sponsorships?

Short answer: there is no official industry threshold. Programs publish either no minimum or a floor of 25,000 to 50,000. Here is what is open at each list size, using only published terms.

What the programs actually say

  • beehiiv Ad Network: paid plan and active sending; CPM or CPC set per campaign.
  • Kit sponsorships: paid plan; pays per unique verified click; no minimum stated.
  • SparkLoop: no defined minimum, suggests 500 to 1,000; brands pay $2 to $20 per new subscriber per SparkLoop's page.
  • Passionfroot: no minimum published; profiles are vetted with a B2B focus, and a “Limited” profile stays off the marketplace but can still receive direct requests.
  • Paved Ad Network: 50,000 subscribers.

FAQ

How many subscribers do I need for newsletter sponsorships?

There is no single official number. Of the programs checked, only Paved's Ad Network (50,000) and EmailNewsletter.ad (25,000+ target) publish a floor. beehiiv, Kit, SparkLoop and Passionfroot publish no hard minimum.

Can I get sponsors with under 1,000 subscribers?

Programs such as the beehiiv Ad Network, Kit sponsorships and the SparkLoop Partner Network publish no hard minimum, though beehiiv and Kit require a paid plan and SparkLoop suggests 500 to 1,000 subscribers first.
