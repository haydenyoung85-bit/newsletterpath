How to choose

  • Just starting, no revenue yet: stay on either free plan. Neither can run sponsorship ads on Free.
  • Want the cheapest paid step: Kit Creator at $39/mo vs beehiiv Lite at $59/mo (monthly billing).
  • Plan to charge readers: beehiiv keeps 0% of subscription revenue; Kit keeps 0.6%. At small revenue the difference is cents.

FAQ

Is Kit or beehiiv cheaper under 1,000 subscribers?

Both have free plans (Kit up to 10,000 subscribers, beehiiv up to 2,500), so both cost $0 under 1,000 subscribers. Paid plans at 1,000 subscribers: beehiiv Lite $59/mo, Kit Creator $39/mo, both on monthly billing.

Can I use the beehiiv Ad Network on the free plan?

No. beehiiv's pricing page shows no Ad Network or paid subscriptions on Free; Lite and up include them.

Which charges less on paid newsletter subscriptions?

beehiiv takes 0% plus Stripe's fee. Kit takes 3.5% + $0.30 including card processing, which its pricing page describes as Kit keeping 0.6%.
