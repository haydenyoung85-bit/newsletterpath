Methodology and disclosure

What counts as verified

A number is shown only if we read it on the vendor's own pricing, help-center or terms page (or rendered the pricing slider to read it). Each row carries its source links and the date we checked it (2026-10-24). If a figure is missing, we write “not published” or leave it blank. We do not estimate.

How prices are chosen

  • “At 1,000 / 5,000” is the cheapest plan that covers that list size, in USD per month, monthly billing unless you switch to yearly.
  • Yearly means the yearly price divided by 12.
  • Some sliders skip sizes. Buttondown has no 5,000 step, so we show the 4,800 tier. Ghost Starter stops at 1,000 members, so the 5,000 figure is Publisher.
  • Flodesk 5,000 and annual prices come from the rendered price list on its page, while the 1,000 monthly prices were confirmed on screen.

Programs

Minimum subscribers, revenue share and payout terms come from each program's help center, pricing page or terms. Rates paid by advertisers vary by campaign and are not promised anywhere here.

Known gaps

  • Kit sponsorships, Swapstack, Passionfroot and Letterhead do not publish payout schedules we could quote.
  • Paid-subscription fees for Mailchimp and EmailOctopus are not published, because we found no paid-newsletter feature on their pricing pages.
  • Which plan unlocks paid newsletters on Kit and GetResponse is flagged as unconfirmed.

Affiliate disclosure

NewsletterPath may earn a commission if you sign up through some outbound links, at no cost to you. Today the links go to the vendors' plain official pages and no affiliate links are active. If that changes, those links will carry rel="sponsored", and this section will say which vendors. Commissions never change a figure, sort order or recommendation on the site.

Corrections

Found something out of date? Check the vendor page linked on the row; the weekly check also logs changes on the changes page.
