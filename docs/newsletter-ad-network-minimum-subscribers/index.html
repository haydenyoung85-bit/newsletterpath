Newsletter ad network minimum subscribers

Short answer: most programs publish no hard minimum. The two hard numbers we found are 50,000 (Paved Ad Network) and a 25,000+ target (EmailNewsletter.ad). SparkLoop suggests 500 to 1,000.

Read this before you rely on “no minimum”

  • “Not published” is not “no limit”. Marketplaces such as Passionfroot vet profiles; audience size is one input, B2B fit matters more.
  • beehiiv and Kit tie their programs to paid plans, so the real cost of entry is the plan price (see fees).
  • Click- and subscriber-based programs pay little until your list is engaged and sized well past these floors. The pages we checked publish no earnings estimates, so we give none.

FAQ

Does the beehiiv Ad Network have a minimum subscriber count?

No minimum is published. It is available on any paid plan to publishers actively sending on beehiiv.

What is Paved's minimum?

At least 50,000 subscribers for the Paved Ad Network.

What is the minimum for EmailNewsletter.ad?

You can apply at any size, but it is built primarily for 25,000+ subscribers and every newsletter is reviewed.

Does SparkLoop require a minimum?

No defined minimum; SparkLoop suggests waiting until you have 500 to 1,000 subscribers.
