beehiiv Lite $59 @1k · Lite $109 @5k

  • Yearly billing, per month: $49 @1k · $95 @5k

  • Lite includes the beehiiv Ad Network, paid subscriptions (1 tier) and paid recommendations. Pro ($109/mo at 1k, $199/mo at 5k, monthly billing) adds the sponsorship storefront.
